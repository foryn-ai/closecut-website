Common pressure patterns (new)

These patterns often show up when you are near the edge. The work is to find your version and change the sequence.

Threat cycle: “we are done” appears in conflict, then gets walked back, then returns again.

Scorekeeping: past injuries become a ledger, so every new conflict pulls in old evidence.

Pursue and withdraw: one person pushes for certainty, the other disengages to avoid pressure.

Repair gap: conflict ends from exhaustion, not repair, so the next fight starts hotter.

Silent decision making: one person is making internal decisions without saying them, then drops them as a shock.

Constraint trap: practical limits keep you together, but the relationship has no shared plan.

In the intensive, we turn these patterns into decision points and agreed scripts for what to do when the pattern starts.

Clarity does not always feel easy, but it reduces chaos and restores agency.
